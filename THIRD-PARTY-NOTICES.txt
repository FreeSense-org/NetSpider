5. IEEE OUI registry data
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NetSpider includes a compressed copy of the IEEE Registration Authority's public
MA-L (OUI) listing (oui.txt), used to map MAC address prefixes to vendor names.
The data is published by the IEEE Registration Authority at
https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.txt and is the property of the IEEE and
the respective assignees. It is included for informational lookup only;
vendor names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The IEEE does not
endorse NetSpider. The listing may be outdated or incomplete.

6. Npcap (NOT distributed with NetSpider)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Packet capture and injection require Npcap (https://npcap.com), developed by the
Nmap Project / Nmap Software LLC. Npcap is NOT included in NetSpider's
installers, MSI or portable zip, and NetSpider does not redistribute it. Its
license does not permit redistribution without a separate OEM agreement.

When Npcap is missing, NetSpider can download the official installer directly
from https://npcap.com and launch it after verifying its Authenticode signature;
you install Npcap separately and accept its own license
(https://npcap.com/oem/redist.html, https://github.com/nmap/npcap/blob/master/LICENSE).
NetSpider talks to Npcap through SharpPcap at run time only.
